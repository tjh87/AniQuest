# Third-party notices

This export retains the project's code and source records. It does not assign a new blanket licence to original AniQuest content or to photographs. Third-party packages and media remain subject to their own terms.

## Open-source dependencies

Exact package versions/integrities are in `package-lock.json`. The installed dependency inventory and available licence texts are in `docs/licenses/DEPENDENCIES.json` and `docs/licenses/THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt`, generated by `node scripts/generate-notices.mjs`. The inventory includes development packages as well as runtime packages; optional platform packages not installed on the build machine may be absent. Retain upstream notices when changing or redistributing dependencies.

Key UI components include React/React DOM, Cytoscape.js, PhotoSwipe, Radix/Base UI, Lucide, Recharts, Vite and Tailwind. Refer to the generated inventory for versions and declared licences rather than assuming all packages share one licence.

The Windows runtime is the official Node.js v22.23.3 x64 distribution. Its original `runtime/windows-x64/LICENSE`, README, changelog, npm and other bundled notices remain in the prepared ZIP. Its archive was verified against the official SHA-256 in `WINDOWS_SETUP.md`. Runtime files are not committed in Git.

## Photographs and source material

Existing local animal images are preserved without cropping or artistic alteration. Original captions, photographer attribution, source links and recorded licence URLs remain in `app/profile-photos.ts` and related metadata JSON. Per-profile records are also collected in `docs/handoff/OFFLINE_INVENTORY.json`.

The package includes 51 local profile photographs already present in the recovered source. Another 90 records point to external photographs and currently lack declared reuse licences. Those external bytes are not downloaded or redistributed by this export; the offline edition displays an explanation and preserves credit/source links. Verify permission or obtain an appropriate licensed replacement before localising more media.

Factual summaries retain their supporting source URLs. Packaging is not a new editorial review, and a publicly accessible webpage is not a blanket grant to copy its images or full text.
