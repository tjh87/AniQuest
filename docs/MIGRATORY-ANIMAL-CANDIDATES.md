# AniQuest migratory-bird expansion

Reviewed: **14 September 2026**. All nine animals are now complete AniQuest profiles. Bird Society of Singapore classifies each bird as a migrant in Singapore.

| Animal | Singapore occurrence | Main Singapore habitats | Singapore RDB3 | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Common sandpiper (*Actitis hypoleucos*) | Very common migrant | Canals, reservoirs, ponds, mudflats and shores | VU | [Bird Society of Singapore](https://singaporebirds.com/species/common-sandpiper/) |
| Barn swallow (*Hirundo rustica*) | Very common migrant | Open ground, wetlands, coast and grassland | NT | [Bird Society of Singapore](https://singaporebirds.com/species/barn-swallow/) |
| Asian brown flycatcher (*Muscicapa dauurica*) | Very common migrant | Forest, parks, gardens and mangroves | LC | [Bird Society of Singapore](https://singaporebirds.com/species/asian-brown-flycatcher/) |
| Arctic warbler (*Phylloscopus borealis*) | Very common migrant | Forest, mangroves, parks and gardens | LC | [Bird Society of Singapore](https://singaporebirds.com/species/arctic-warbler/) |
| Brown shrike (*Lanius cristatus*) | Common migrant | Parks, grassland and other open areas | VU | [Bird Society of Singapore](https://singaporebirds.com/species/brown-shrike/) |
| Blue-tailed bee-eater (*Merops philippinus*) | Common migrant | Open areas, marshes, forest and watersides | LC | [Bird Society of Singapore](https://singaporebirds.com/species/blue-tailed-bee-eater/) |
| Pacific golden plover (*Pluvialis fulva*) | Common migrant | Fields, freshwater marshes, beaches and mudflats | VU | [Bird Society of Singapore](https://singaporebirds.com/species/pacific-golden-plover/) |
| Common redshank (*Tringa totanus*) | Common migrant | Mudflats, sandflats and brackish lagoons | VU | [Bird Society of Singapore](https://singaporebirds.com/species/common-redshank/) |
| Yellow-rumped flycatcher (*Ficedula zanthopygia*) | Common migrant | Forest, parks, gardens and mangroves | LC | [Bird Society of Singapore](https://singaporebirds.com/species/yellow-rumped-flycatcher/) |

National categories use the [NParks Singapore bird list and RDB3 table](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/nature/species-list/birds).

Each profile has a credited photograph, two sourced facts, a current Singapore category, a seasonal chart note and six learning checks. The seasonal periods come from reviewed weekly occurrence data. They are not fixed arrival or departure dates.
